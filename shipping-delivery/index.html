Shipping, Freight & Delivery Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: September 2026

CD Group manages central warehouse operations from Maligaon Gate No-3, Guwahati, Assam. We provide scheduled freight transport, full-truckload (FTL), and less-than-truckload (LTL) deliveries across all eight states of Northeast India.

1. Estimated Transit Timelines

  • Guwahati & Metro Assam: Same-day or 24-hour dispatch
  • Upper & Lower Assam Districts: 24 to 48 hours
  • Meghalaya (Shillong, Tura, Jowai): 24 to 48 hours
  • Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Manipur, Tripura: 36 to 72 hours
  • Mizoram & Remote High-Altitude Corridors: 48 to 96 hours

2. Freight Terms & Waybills

Freight is dispatched via GST-registered commercial transport carriers with mandatory e-Way bill compliance. Delivery charges may be pre-quoted or consigned on TO-PAY basis as specified in the formal proforma quote.

3. Transit Insurance & Inspection

Receiving stockists are requested to inspect physical seals and carton counts at the time of delivery before signing transport lorry receipts (LR).
