Frequently Asked Questions

Manipur B2B Procurement FAQs

What is the transit time from Guwahati to Imphal and Moreh?

Standard truck freight to Imphal takes 36 to 48 hours via NH-37 / NH-2. Forward dispatch to Moreh border post typically takes an additional 24 hours.

Can we order specialized excavator spares for landslide emergency clearance?

Yes, we maintain priority emergency dispatch stock for JCB and excavator wear parts, hydraulic seal kits, and hoses for infrastructure clearance contractors.

Do you supply bulk packaged Assam tea for Manipur distributors?

Yes, we provide bulk garden-direct Assam CTC tea in 35kg/50kg woven sacks as well as consumer-ready branded tea pouches.
