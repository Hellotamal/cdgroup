Arunachal Pradesh B2B Procurement FAQs

What is the delivery timeline for high-altitude locations like Tawang or Bomdila?

Dispatch to Itanagar and Pasighat takes 24–48 hours. Shipments to Tawang, Bomdila, and upper Siang take 48–72 hours depending on weather conditions and mountain passes.

Do you supply lubricants that remain fluid in low temperatures?

Yes, we supply specialized low-viscosity, low-pour-point hydraulic and engine oils rated for severe sub-zero cold start performance in high-altitude construction sites.

Can project contractors order combined shipments of spares and food supplies?

Yes, our central Guwahati warehouse can consolidate machinery spares, oils, and FMCG food inventory into unified vehicle loads for efficient freight delivery.
