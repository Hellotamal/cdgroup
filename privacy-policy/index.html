Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: September 2026

CD Group ("we", "our", or "us"), headquartered at Maligaon Gate No-3, NFR Railway HQ, Guwahati, Assam - 781011, is committed to respecting the privacy of our business-to-business (B2B) clients, wholesale stockists, contractors, and website visitors.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a Request for Quotation (RFQ), register for a B2B stockist account, or contact our sales department, we collect business contact details including: Company Name, Contact Person Name, Business Email Address, Mobile/Phone Number, GSTIN, Shipping Address, and Product Specification Requirements.

2. How We Use Business Information

Information collected is used strictly for B2B commercial operations: processing RFQ pricing proposals, processing freight dispatch orders, verifying GST tax compliance, and communicating order updates.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your corporate information to third parties. Data is shared strictly with authorized logistics carriers and statutory tax authorities as required by Indian law.
